Part 7

How I Work With AI

When Claude Code was released last year, I felt i needed to learn. This will speed up my work as a programmer and widen my knowledge on some other tech tools I haven't exposed in the past. Now, AI is rapidly changing & evolving. I use AI as my speedy assistant but i am the decision maker. To illustrate: AI is my supercar and I am that cool driver. I don't need to know how to be a mechanic just to run this supercar. I rely relatively on its efficiency, comfort and looks. But still needs to gas up at a certain mile. That's AI. Humans created AI, that makes human far superior. So while AI makes my work easy, i do the judgment and i make AI follow my direction. And yes, i always have in mind when Claude says: "Claude is AI and can make mistakes. Please double-check responses."

I usually begin my design with Claude Chat. In plain language, i freely describe what i like following the rule: Assign a role, Provide clear context, give examples if necessary and state dependecies/limits. Once that done, i usually make notes on the created docs until i get all the core functionalities and when i am sure it matches with the business requirement based on the data i gathered. I'd then transfer that to chatgpt and let it attack. After refinements made, both me and AI, i will then ask Claude Code to create the application b y providing the right stack to build on.

AI acts as an agentic terminal tool that directly manages fiels, executes commands and handles complete dev workflows.
